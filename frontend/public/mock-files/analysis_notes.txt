SCRAP analysis notes — COVID-19 PBMC (Wilk et al. 2020, GSE150728)

Dataset
- 44,649 cells after QC, 13 donors (7 COVID-19, 6 healthy)
- Counts normalized to 10k per cell, log1p; HVGs: 3,000

Checks the critic applied
- Pseudobulk by donor before differential expression
- Benjamini-Hochberg correction across genes
- Effect sizes reported (log2 fold change)
- Donor and batch confounding checked; >= 30 cells per donor and group

Findings so far
1. Interferon-stimulated genes up in CD14 monocytes (IFI27, ISG15, MX1).
2. HLA class II genes down in CD14 monocytes (HLA-DRA log2FC -1.1).
3. Developing-neutrophil cluster mostly in ventilated patients (needs revision).
